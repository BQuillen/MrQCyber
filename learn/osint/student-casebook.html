Someone Who Knows Us

Casey Selvin’s family received messages that seemed to know too much. Find what was publicly available and explain how disconnected details could make an impostor sound familiar.

Your starting information: Casey Selvin · Lexington.
Case date: 25 October 2026.

Open the investigation

Your assignment

  1. Find the correct Casey. Explain how you ruled out at least one other person.
  2. Follow the public connections to an adult in Casey’s life.
  3. Collect supported findings from at least three kinds of sources. Aim for 10–12 well-explained findings, including two corroborated across sources.
  4. Resolve one outdated or contradictory detail.
  5. Inspect a downloadable original photo. Explain what its metadata does and does not establish.
  6. Write an annotated, simulated social-engineering message and a defensive response plan.

Investigation boundaries

All characters and sources are fictional. Remain inside the training environment. Do not contact people, send your draft, enter real passwords, or look for real private information. Your work is an explanation of exposure, not a claim that someone deserves to be deceived.
