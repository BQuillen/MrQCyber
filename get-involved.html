Get Involved

Whether you're an industry professional, researcher, educator, or community member, there are meaningful ways to support the next generation of cybersecurity talent. Tell us how you'd like to contribute.

🎤
Guest Speaker

Share your expertise with students. We welcome professionals from all areas of cybersecurity, technology, and adjacent fields to speak about careers, industry trends, or specific technical topics.

🤝
Industry Partnership

Partner with us to offer internship pipelines, tool access, sponsored competitions, or curriculum input. Help shape what the next generation of professionals knows and can do.

💡
Resource & Mentorship

Donate lab equipment, software licenses, or your time as a mentor. Even occasional mentorship sessions make a significant impact on students exploring a future in this field.
