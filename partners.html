Program Partners

Our program is strengthened by relationships with industry leaders and academic institutions who provide resources, mentorship, and real-world opportunities for our students. Click any partner card to visit their website.

Industry Partners

Cybersecurity
KC7 Cyber Foundation

KC7 teaches students how to investigate an attack using query language and the Azure Data Explorer. Through this partnership, students will not only play a game to develop their professional skills, but collaborate with security analysts to learn and share their work.

Visit Site →
Cyber Defense
National Guard

The National Guard Cybersecurity team teaches students how cybersecurity is used to defend national environment while also running simulations that teach the importance of cybersecurity in their lives.

Visit Site →
Red Teaming
Red Siege

Red Siege is a offensive cybersecurity organization that uses ethical hacking to help organizations secure their physical and digital locations. Our partner shares experiences with students to round out their knowledge of cybersecurity defense.

Visit Site →
Training
Microsoft - Project Bayfield

Microsoft's Project Bayfield, typically intended for university students, provides instruction on cybersecurity topics that revolve around security operations centers and business continuity. This partnership will provide certifications to students who are ready to jump into the workforce.
